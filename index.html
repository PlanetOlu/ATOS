ATOS Organic Health Options

SCRIPT ON RED — ACCENT USE

Clear Space

Always maintain clear space equal to the height of the leaf icon around all logo versions. Never crowd with other elements.

Minimum Size

Primary logo: never below 140px wide digital / 35mm print. Use monogram mark below that threshold.

Approved Colors

Primary on white and light backgrounds. Reversed on forest green. Red version for accent CTAs only — never as primary identity.

What Not To Do

Do not stretch, rotate, recolor, add drop shadows, or place on photographic backgrounds without a solid overlay.

02 — Color

THE PALETTE

ATOS Red #D42B2B CTA · Urgency · Action
Deep Red #A81A1A Premium · Emphasis
Pure White #FFFFFF Background · Clean Space

Primary Use

Healer's Forest (#1A4737) dominates all brand touchpoints — backgrounds, headers, navbars. White provides breathing room and clean contrast. Never use both light colors together without a green anchor.

Content Ratio

60% Forest & White · 25% Sage & Leaf · 15% ATOS Red. This keeps the feed premium and health-focused without feeling cold or clinical.

Red = Action

ATOS Red (#D42B2B) is reserved exclusively for CTAs, booking prompts, and urgent moments. It creates strong contrast against forest green and signals that something requires action.

05 — Pattern

BRAND PATTERN

ATOSA TOSAT OSATO SATOS
DARK — ON FOREST GREEN
ATOSA TOSAT OSATO SATOS
LIGHT — ON WHITE

How to Use

Apply as background on social posts, story covers, highlight thumbnails, and merchandise. Always 10–15% opacity to keep it subtle.

Variations

Full ATOS sequence (shown) or "A" monogram repeated. Leaf icon pattern variant available for organic content moments.

Never Do

Never at full opacity. Never on busy photographic backgrounds. Pattern is always texture — not the primary visual element.

06 — Voice & Tone

BRAND VOICE

01 Pillar One

EXPERT & TRUSTWORTHY

20+ years means quiet authority. We demonstrate expertise through specific, useful knowledge — not credentials on a wall.

02 Pillar Two

WARM & HUMAN

Mrs. Okoli's clients feel heard, not lectured. The brand speaks like a knowledgeable friend — direct, caring, individual-focused.

03 Pillar Three

RESULTS-FIRST

Every communication leads back to transformation. Not "eating healthy" — reversing conditions, gaining energy, healing bodies.

✦ Do Say

  • Specific: "For diabetics, ugwu leaf lowers blood sugar by..."
  • Personal: "Your body can heal. It just needs the right fuel."
  • Results: "Clients with HBP see changes within 6–8 weeks."
  • Empowering: "You already have what it takes."
  • Nigerian-rooted: garden egg, oha, plantain, ogi, waterleaf
  • Action: "Book a consultation." "DM 'PLAN' to start." Clear CTAs.

✗ Don't Say

  • Vague: "Live your best life." "Glow from within."
  • Fear-based: "If you don't change, you'll get worse."
  • Overly clinical: "Macronutrient ratios and glycemic index..."
  • Trendy but empty: "Clean eating." "Detox." "Superfood."
  • Western food focus: always tie back to Nigerian ingredients
  • Uncertain: "might help", "could possibly", "you may want to..."
